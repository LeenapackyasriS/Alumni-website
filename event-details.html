About This Event

Join fellow alumni for a day filled with reunions, campus tours and celebration. Alumni Meet 2026 brings together graduates across batches for networking sessions, felicitation of achievers, cultural performances and a formal dinner. Whether you graduated a year ago or three decades ago, this is your chance to reconnect with classmates, meet faculty and relive campus memories.
